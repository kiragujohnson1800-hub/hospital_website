Our story

Good care starts with listening.

BlossomCare Hospital is dedicated to providing compassionate, affordable and quality medical services to individuals and families in Nakuru and the surrounding community.

Healthcare is personal. We take time to listen, explain your options and support you through every step of your care. Our aim is to make every visit feel welcoming and every decision feel informed.
